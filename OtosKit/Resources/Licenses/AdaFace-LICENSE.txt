AdaFace IR-101 (WebFace12M), CVLFace pretrained model
https://github.com/mk-minchul/CVLface
https://huggingface.co/minchul/cvlface_adaface_ir101_webface12m

AdaFace: Quality Adaptive Margin for Face Recognition.
Minchul Kim, Anil K. Jain, Xiaoming Liu. CVPR 2022. arXiv:2204.00964.

Otos ships these weights converted to Core ML (AdaFaceIR101.mlpackage) with 8-bit linear
per-channel weight quantization; the conversion and quantization modify the weights.

The model card asks users to follow the licence of the training dataset (WebFace260M,
non-commercial research). Otos is a non-commercial application.
